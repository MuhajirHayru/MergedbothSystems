# GreenPath landing page — Canva design brief

## Goal

Design a calm, confident, mobile-friendly farm-to-market landing page that is immediately understood by Ethiopian farmers, wholesalers, and retailers. Keep the page visual and uncluttered. The user should see three connected moments: growing, transporting, and ordering.

## Visual direction

- Use authentic, warm documentary photography with natural daylight and real agricultural settings.
- Palette: deep forest green `#173B2B`, harvest green `#17633C`, soft cream `#FAF9F5`, and muted leaf green `#C6E5B5`.
- Use a highly legible sans-serif family, large short headlines, restrained supporting copy, and generous whitespace.
- Keep photography full-frame and correctly composed. Do not crop off the farmer, whole truck, or produce load.
- Design desktop first, then adapt the same hierarchy for narrow mobile screens.

## Header

- Compact GreenPath wordmark on the left.
- Only two account actions on the right: **Sign in** and **Create account**.
- Cream background, simple spacing, no extra navigation links or utility controls.

## Scene 1 — Farmer at work

- Full-width, near-viewport-height opening image of a farmer actively harvesting or tending vegetables in a field.
- Let the photograph fill the background. Place a dark, soft gradient behind the copy for legibility.
- Headline: **Good food starts with farmers in the field.**
- Supporting copy: **GreenPath connects the people who grow fresh produce with the buyers who need it.**
- Keep account actions in the header; avoid adding a second set of prominent buttons over the photo.

## Scene 2 — Produce in transit

- Green section with a wide, full-frame photograph showing the entire large truck from a little distance, plus its loaded cargo.
- The truck must be a conventional four-wheel-or-larger vehicle, not a three-wheeler. The visible load should clearly include tomatoes and potatoes.
- Do not crop away the truck cab, wheels, or produce. Keep the truck and load readable on mobile too.
- Overlay exactly this copy and no other section copy: **Your order is on the way**.

## Scene 3 — Farmers and phone orders

- Large photo of farmers smiling together while checking a smartphone in the field; the phone should be visible.
- Pair the image with a short text block explaining that farmers check their GreenPath dashboard and buyers can send orders directly to them.
- Keep the text and image visually balanced and avoid decorative dashboard mockups that make the phone unreadable.

## Footer

- A compact forest-green footer with the configured GreenPath contact email, phone, and office location.
- No additional menus, promotional blocks, or unrelated links.

## Interaction and accessibility notes for implementation

- **Sign in** continues to `/login`; **Create account** continues to `/register`.
- Maintain strong text contrast over every photograph, descriptive image alt text, keyboard focus visibility, and reduced-motion-safe transitions.
- Use responsive image positioning so the farmer, truck, and load remain visible on small screens.
